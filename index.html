Welcome

Your Home in the Mountains

Tucked into the snowy hillsides above La Clusaz, this traditional chalet pairs rustic timber charm with genuine alpine comfort. Thick stone foundations and wide wooden balconies look out over pine forest and mountain ridgeline, while inside, warm pine-clad bedrooms and a wood-burning fire keep the cold firmly outside.

It's an easy base for a ski week with the family — bunk rooms for the kids, a choice of cozy double bedrooms for the grown-ups, a full kitchen, and a village and lift system just a short drive or walk away. Come summer, the same chalet becomes a peaceful hiking retreat surrounded by green alpine meadows.

Beyond the chalet

Things to Do in La Clusaz

From high-mountain hikes to family-friendly fairways, La Clusaz keeps the whole group busy well beyond ski season.

Mountain Biking

La Clusaz Bike Park spans around 125km of marked descents and trails for every level, open through the summer season.

E-Biking

Rental e-MTBs make the climbs easy, opening up the surrounding valleys and higher trails without the legwork.

Golf des Confins

A scenic mountain golf course up on the Confins plateau, with the Aravis peaks as a backdrop to every hole.

Mini Golf

A 12-hole mini-golf course by the Confins chapel, open all day with no booking needed — easy fun for the kids.

Trou de la Mouche

A dramatic high-mountain loop through the Aravis range — a demanding but unforgettable day hike for experienced walkers.

Lac des Confins

A gentle, family-friendly lake a short walk from the village, perfect for a picnic or an easy flat walk.

Where you'll be

Location

La Clusaz is a traditional Savoyard village in the Aravis mountain range, an hour from Geneva and Annecy. Known for its family-friendly slopes in winter and its hiking and cycling trails in summer, it keeps the lived-in charm of a real alpine village rather than a purpose-built resort.

  • La Clusaz village centre — short drive
  • Nearest ski lift — a few minutes away
  • Geneva Airport — approx. 1 hour
  • Annecy — approx. 35 minutes
